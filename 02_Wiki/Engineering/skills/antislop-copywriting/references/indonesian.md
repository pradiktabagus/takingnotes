# Indonesian copy supplement

Load this when the UI or marketing text is written in Indonesian (or mixes Indonesian and English). The English rules in `SKILL.md` and the core still apply (no invented facts, no fake stats or testimonials, no em dash). This file adds what the English lists miss.

## Register: decide once

- Pick `Anda` (formal, banking, government, B2B) or `kamu` (casual, consumer, creator tools) from the brand, and hold it across the whole product. Do not mix them.
- No `lo/gue` unless the owner's voice sample uses it.
- Prefer active voice with a named actor. "Kami mengirim struk ke email Anda" beats "Struk akan dikirimkan".

## Buzzwords and empty phrases (Indonesian)

Treat these like the English buzzword list (R-16). Say the concrete benefit instead.

- solusi terbaik, solusi terdepan, solusi terpadu, solusi digital, platform all-in-one, one-stop solution
- inovatif, revolusioner, canggih, mutakhir, berkelas dunia, kelas enterprise
- andal, terpercaya, berkualitas tinggi (only with evidence)
- tanpa ribet, mudah dan cepat, dalam sekejap, seamless, powerful, efektif dan efisien
- transformasi digital, ekosistem, memberdayakan, tingkatkan produktivitas Anda, wujudkan impian Anda, naik level

## Generic CTAs (Indonesian)

Avoid as default (R-15): Mulai Sekarang, Pelajari Lebih Lanjut, Coba Sekarang, Jelajahi, Temukan, Klik di Sini, Selengkapnya.

Name the action and, where true, the cost or outcome:

- "Buat akun gratis", "Lihat demo langsung", "Unduh untuk Android", "Hubungi tim lewat WhatsApp", "Bandingkan paket harga"
- Only claim "gratis" or "tanpa kartu kredit" if it is true.

## AI-sounding patterns in Indonesian

- Openers and fillers: "Di era digital ini", "Di dunia yang semakin...", "Tidak dapat dipungkiri bahwa", "Sudah saatnya Anda...", "Tahukah Anda?", "Mari kita jelajahi".
- Closers: "Kesimpulannya", "Dengan demikian", "Semoga bermanfaat", "Jangan ragu untuk menghubungi kami" in product UI.
- Rule of three: "cepat, mudah, dan aman" with no evidence for any of them.
- Negative parallelism: "bukan hanya X, tetapi juga Y".
- Aphorisms: "X adalah kunci kesuksesan", "Kualitas adalah prioritas kami".
- Stiff nominalization: "melakukan pembayaran" (use "membayar"), "melakukan pendaftaran" (use "mendaftar"), "dapat dilakukan" chains.
- Padding: "berbagai macam", "sangat amat", "pada dasarnya", "dapat dikatakan bahwa".
- Inflated significance: "merevolusi cara Anda...", "mengubah selamanya".

## Mixed Indonesian and English

- Keep English terms that Indonesian users actually use (login, download, chat, email, notifikasi). Do not translate them stiffly.
- For UI labels pick one family and keep it: "Masuk / Daftar / Keluar" or "Login / Sign up / Logout". Do not mix inside one screen. Be consistent with "Unggah" vs "Upload".
- Do not sprinkle English buzzwords into Indonesian sentences to sound modern.

## Formats and conventions

- Currency: `Rp 1.500.000` (dot as thousands separator, "Rp" followed by a space). Decimals use a comma: `4,5`.
- Dates: `28 Oktober 2026` or `28 Okt 2026`; avoid ambiguous `10/11/26`.
- Time: 24-hour with zone: `14.30 WIB` (WIB, WITA, WIT).
- Phone: `+62 812 3456 7890` or `0812-3456-7890`; consistent.
- Use real numbers only (R-17). If data is missing, use `[DATA ASLI]`.

## UI messages

- Errors say what happened and what to do: "Kata sandi salah. Coba lagi atau atur ulang kata sandi." Not "Terjadi kesalahan. Silakan coba beberapa saat lagi." as a catch-all.
- Empty states say what goes here and how to start: "Belum ada pesanan. Pesanan Anda akan muncul di sini."
- Buttons: verb first, short, specific ("Simpan perubahan", "Kirim ulang kode").

## Before and after (illustrative style, no product facts)

- Before: "Solusi digital terdepan untuk meningkatkan produktivitas Anda." After: state what the product does and for whom, in one concrete sentence the owner can verify.
- Before: "Mulai Sekarang". After: "Buat akun gratis" (if free).
- Before: "Di era digital ini, keamanan data sangat penting." After: delete it and start with the actual feature.
